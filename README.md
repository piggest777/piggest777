# Denis Rakitin

I’m an iOS developer in Stockholm. I build apps in Swift and SwiftUI, with a focus on connecting them to speakers, headphones, and other devices over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

## At Marshall Group

I helped take a new iOS app from initial development through release. My work includes Bluetooth Low Energy discovery and connection management, over-the-air firmware update flows, and Wi-Fi reconnection using Bonjour and asynchronous connection handling. [Marshall Bluetooth on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/marshall-bluetooth/id1351654161)

I’ve also refactored device setup flows using SwiftUI and Swift Concurrency, built a Figma plugin to speed up localization, and developed and deployed an internal inventory app.
